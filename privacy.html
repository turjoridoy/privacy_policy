Privacy Policy

Sport Time · Last updated 20 November 2026

Sport Time is a team randomizer and match timer for casual sport. You enter the people in your squad, the app splits them into balanced teams, and an optional timer runs the match clock.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no accounts, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

So your squad is still there next time you open the app, the following is saved in the app's own private storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Player names you type or paste To keep your squad between sessions
Skill ratings (1–5), star marks, and who is playing today To draw balanced teams
Settings: language, sport format, team names and colours, keep-apart rules, timer options To remember how you like the app set up
The most recent team drawSo it is still on screen when you return

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app, or at any time from inside the app by clearing your squad and restoring default settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts — player names are only what you type or paste in yourself
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind
  • No usage statistics, crash reporting, or device fingerprinting

Sharing teams

When you tap Copy or Share, the app builds a plain-text list of the drawn teams and hands it to your device's own clipboard or share sheet. You choose where it goes. Nothing is uploaded by the app, and the text deliberately excludes players' skill ratings, so sharing teams never reveals how anyone was rated.

Permissions

PermissionUsed for
VIBRATE Optional vibration when the match timer warns or ends. Can be switched off in settings.

The app also plays short sound files bundled with it, which needs no permission. The app requests no network permission and has no network functionality.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
